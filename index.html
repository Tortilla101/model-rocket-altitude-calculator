How this calculator works (Physics Engine Details)
Numerical Integration: Flight is simulated via Semi-Implicit Euler integration at 3000 steps per second of burn. Mass updates continuously as propellant burns.

Dynamic Weathercocking: Instead of locking a static angle, the simulation dynamically interpolates the rocket's nose toward the relative wind vector after leaving the rail, replicating real restoring forces (fins correcting trajectory).

Atmospheric & Gravity Models: - Wind Shear: Applies the atmospheric power law. Wind speed increases logarithmically with altitude.
- Temperature Lapse: Temperature drops by 6.5°C per 1km altitude, continuously recalculating moist-air density.
- Dynamic Gravity: Uses inverse-square law to slightly decrease gravitational pull as altitude increases.
